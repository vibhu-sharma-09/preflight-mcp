Preflight

Checks a marketing email before it goes out: links, tracking, personalisation, accessibility and compliance.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded or stored.

Paste the HTML from your email platform, or the raw source of a test send.

Columns: campaign, sent, delivered, unique_opens, unique_clicks, bounces, unsubscribes.
